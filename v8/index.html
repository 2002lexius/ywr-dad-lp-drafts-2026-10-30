For Future Dads

“As a mom, I picked this up hoping it would help my husband feel more prepared for our first baby, and I ended up reading much of it myself. I honestly wish more dads would read a book like this. What I appreciated most is that it doesn't just focus on diapers and hospital bags. It encourages fathers to understand what their partner is experiencing physically and emotionally, while also acknowledging that becoming a dad can be scary. I loved seeing the emphasis on communication, supporting your partner, looking after mental health, and becoming an active parent instead of just a bystander. It feels like the author genuinely wants dads to be confident, involved, and compassionate from pregnancy onward. The writing is approachable and often funny, which makes it an easy read. There were several moments that made me smile because they captured the reality of pregnancy surprisingly well. As a mom, it was reassuring to see my experience validated while also giving practical advice to help fathers know what they can actually do. It's not perfect. Some of the humor and sports analogies won't resonate with everyone, and there are a few moments where the tone leans a little more casual than I personally prefer. There are also occasional product mentions that felt a bit promotional. None of those things took away from the overall value of the book for me. Overall, this is a thoughtful, encouraging guide that helps first-time dads become true partners during pregnancy and beyond. If you're expecting your first baby—or you're a mom looking for a book to give your husband—I would absolutely recommend this one. It offers practical advice, reassurance, and plenty of reminders that showing up with love and support matters more than being perfect.”

Lauren Arditti · Judge.me
