“As a mom, I picked this up hoping it would help my husband feel more prepared for our first baby, and I ended up reading much of it myself. I honestly wish more dads would read a book like this. It encourages fathers to understand what their partner is experiencing physically and emotionally, while also acknowledging that becoming a dad can be scary. I loved seeing the emphasis on communication, supporting your partner, looking after mental health, and becoming an active parent instead of just a bystander. It feels like the author genuinely wants dads to be confident, involved, and compassionate from pregnancy onward. The writing is approachable and often funny, which makes it an easy read. There were several moments that made me smile because they captured the reality of pregnancy surprisingly well. Overall, this is a thoughtful, encouraging guide that helps first-time dads become true partners during pregnancy and beyond.
